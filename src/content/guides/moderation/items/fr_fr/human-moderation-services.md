Human Moderation Services is managed moderation by the FastComments team. Our moderators work your queue, in your dashboard, under your guidelines. There is no separate tool and no export of your data to a third party: the service runs on the moderation features every account already has.

### Comment ça fonctionne

1. **Nous convenons des règles.** You share your community guidelines and tell us what to approve, remove, and escalate. Both sides sign off on a written playbook before anyone touches your queue.
2. **Nous rejoignons votre compte.** FastComments moderators are added through [Adding Moderators](/guide-moderation.html#moderators-adding) and scoped with [Moderation Groups](/guide-moderation.html#moderation-groups), so they only see the pages, categories, or sites you assign.
3. **Les machines d’abord, les humains ensuite.** [Spam Detection](/guide-moderation.html#spam-detection) and [AI Agents](/guide-ai-agents.html) handle the obvious cases. Our moderators work what is left: context, tone, appeals, and anything an agent escalated.
4. **Vous voyez tout.** Each action is recorded in [Comment Logs](/guide-moderation.html#comment-logs) and [Moderator Stats](/guide-moderation.html#moderator-stats) with the moderator's name on it. Policy questions go to your named escalation contact.

### Ce que vous contrôlez

- **Directives.** Our moderators follow the playbook written from your guidelines. When a case is not covered, it is escalated to you rather than decided for you.
- **Portée.** Widen or narrow access at any time by editing the Moderation Group our moderators belong to.
- **Contacts d’escalade.** You name who on your side receives policy questions, and we name who on ours owns the queue.
- **Accès.** [Removing a moderator](/guide-moderation.html#moderators-removing) ends their access immediately.

### Travailler aux côtés des agents IA

Agents and FastComments moderators act through the same tools, so every action shows up in the same logs and the same moderation page. A typical setup runs spam detection and AI Agents as the first pass, with human moderators handling the comments agents flag for review. See [AI Agents](/guide-ai-agents.html) for how agents escalate to a human.

The [trust factor](/guide-moderation.html#spam-detection) ties the two together. Each commenter carries a 0 to 100 score built from tenure, comment count, and pinned comments. Spam detection and AI Agents read it to decide how strictly to treat a comment, and our moderators adjust it by hand when the automatic score is wrong. Every change is recorded in [Comment Logs](/guide-moderation.html#comment-logs), so a human decision today changes how the automated layers treat that person from then on.

### Où se trouvent nos modérateurs

For global and US customers, FastComments moderators are based in the United States, so your data does not leave the country. On the EU deployment, only EU-based moderators work your queue, so comment data does not leave the EU.
