Live Polls let you ask your community a question right in the comments. A poll is attached to a comment, renders inside it, and everyone on the page votes in place without leaving the thread.

Results update live for everyone viewing the page as votes come in, with no refresh needed. Poll authors can set a closing date, hide the results until someone has voted, and choose whether voters stay anonymous.

Polls are built into the FastComments comment widget, so they work on any site where the widget is installed, with no extra code.

[Try the Live Polls demo](https://fastcomments.com/product/live-polls) to vote on a poll and post your own.
