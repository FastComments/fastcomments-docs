The author of a poll is notified as responses come in:

- **In-app**: a notification under the bell icon when the poll gets a new response. When many people vote, these are grouped into one notification.
- **Email**: a daily email to the poll's author when their poll got new responses that day.

Only a person's first vote counts as a new response. Changing a vote does not send a notification, and authors are not notified about their own votes.

Notifications respect the poll's privacy. For anonymous polls they only say how many new responses came in. Voter names are included for public polls, and for admin-visible polls when the author is an admin or moderator.

The email includes a link to stop notifications for that poll. Its content can be changed with the **Poll Response Notification** template, see [Email Templates](/guide-email-templates.html).
