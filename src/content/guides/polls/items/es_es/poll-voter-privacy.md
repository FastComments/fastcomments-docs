Every poll has a privacy setting, chosen by its author when creating it. The poll shows its setting under the options, so voters know who can see their vote before they cast it.

- **Anónimo** (por defecto): only the totals are shown. Nobody, including admins, can see who voted for what.
- **Los administradores pueden ver quién votó**: only admins and moderators can open **Ver quién votó**, which lists each voter and their choice.
- **Público**: any viewer can click **Ver quién votó** and see who voted for what.

Votes from visitors who are not logged in are counted but have no name attached, so they do not appear in the voter list.
