FastComments offers EU-hosted infrastructure for GDPR-friendly deployments. To keep all traffic on the EU endpoints, point both URL settings at the EU hosts.

In the plugin settings:

- Set **CDN URL** to the EU CDN endpoint.
- Set **Site URL** to the EU site endpoint.

If you use the sidebar modules, set each module's **CDN URL** to the EU CDN endpoint as well.

<sup>Note!</sup> Create your account and Tenant on [eu.fastcomments.com](https://eu.fastcomments.com) so your data lives in the EU region. A Tenant created on the US site cannot be served from the EU endpoints.
