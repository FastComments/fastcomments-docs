The FastComments extension needs:

- **Joomla 4.2 or later**, including Joomla 5. The comment plugin works on Joomla 4.0+, but the bundled sidebar modules require Joomla 4.2.
- **PHP 7.4 or later.** Joomla 5 itself requires PHP 8.1 or later.
- A [FastComments](https://fastcomments.com) account for your Tenant ID. Secure SSO also needs your API Secret. A free trial is enough to test the extension; paid plans start at $0.99/mo.

The extension depends only on Joomla core. There are no other extensions or libraries to install.
