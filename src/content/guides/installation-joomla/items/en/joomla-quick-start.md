This is the "too long, didn't read" version of the Joomla instructions.

1. Download the FastComments package, `pkg_fastcomments.zip`.
2. In the Joomla administrator, go to `System > Install > Extensions` and upload the package.
3. Go to `System > Manage > Plugins`, find `FastComments`, and enable it.
4. Open the plugin and enter your Tenant ID from [Settings > API/SSO](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/api) ([EU](https://eu.fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/api)). Add your API Secret if you want Secure SSO.
5. Open any article on your site. Comments appear below the content.

That is the whole setup. The package also installs three optional modules: Recent Comments, Recent Discussions, and Top Pages. Drop them into any template position when you want them.
