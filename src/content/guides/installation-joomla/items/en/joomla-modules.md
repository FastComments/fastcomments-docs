The package installs three optional modules you can place in any template position, such as a sidebar or footer.

| Module | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **FastComments: Recent Comments** | The most recent comments posted across your site. |
| **FastComments: Recent Discussions** | Pages with the most recent discussion activity. |
| **FastComments: Top Pages** | Your most-commented pages. |

## Add a module

1. Go to `Content > Site Modules` and click `New`.
2. Pick one of the `FastComments: ...` modules.
3. Enter your Tenant ID, choose a position, and set the menu assignment.
4. Recent Comments and Recent Discussions let you set how many items to show (1-50). Top Pages shows your most active pages.
5. Save and publish.

<sup>Note!</sup> Modules carry their own Tenant ID and CDN URL, so they work even on pages without the comment widget. Set the same Tenant ID you used in the plugin.
