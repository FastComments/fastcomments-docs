The FastComments app for Webflow lets you add comments, live chat, reviews, and more to any page or CMS Collection Template - no embed code to hand-write.

### Install the app

1. Open the FastComments app on the [Webflow App Marketplace](https://webflow.com/apps) and add it to your site (you can also install it from **Site Settings > Apps & Integrations**).
2. Open your site in the Webflow Designer, press **E** to open the Apps panel, and launch **FastComments**.

The app is free to install. Your comment plan lives on your FastComments account, so you only ever pay FastComments for the plan you choose - Webflow adds no charges of its own.

### Get your Tenant ID

You'll need your **Tenant ID**, a public identifier that is safe to expose on your site. Find it at [My Account > API Keys](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/api-secret).

Want to try it before signing up? Use the Tenant ID `demo`.
