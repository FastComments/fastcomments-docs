### Publish

Click **Publish** in the Webflow Designer.

Comments do not appear on the Designer canvas or in Preview mode - they load only on the published site, once the page is live and the loader script is in place.

Open a published page and your FastComments widget will be there. On a CMS Collection Template, open any two posts: each has its own independent comment thread.

If a widget doesn't appear, confirm the loader script is saved in **Site Settings > Custom Code > Footer Code** and that you've published since adding it. See **Domain Errors** if you see a domain-related message instead of comments.
