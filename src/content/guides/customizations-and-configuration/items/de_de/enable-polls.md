Polls let commenters attach a question with 2 to 10 options to a comment. Everyone on the page votes in place and sees the results update live.

Polls are off by default. Check **Umfragen aktivieren** in the widget customization settings to add a poll button to the comment toolbar. Two more settings then appear:

- **Wer kann Umfragen erstellen**: Admins/Moderators Only (the default), or Everyone.
- **Anonyme Umfrage‑Stimmen zulassen**: when unchecked (the default), only logged in users can vote.
