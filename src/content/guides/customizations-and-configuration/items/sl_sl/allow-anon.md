By default, FastComments will require an email to comment. It does not have to be a valid email, however until the user clicks a link sent to them,
their comment will display an "Unverified Comment" label.

Privzeto FastComments zahteva e‑naslov za komentiranje. Ni nujno, da je e‑naslov veljaven, vendar dokler uporabnik ne klikne povezave, ki mu je poslana, bo njegov komentar prikazan z oznako "Neoverjen komentar".

However, we can remove the email requirement. The email input field will still show, but it will no longer be required.

Vendar lahko odstranimo zahtevo po e‑naslovu. Vnosno polje za e‑naslov bo še vedno prikazano, vendar ne bo več obvezno.
