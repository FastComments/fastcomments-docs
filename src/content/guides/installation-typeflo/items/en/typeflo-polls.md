Once FastComments is on your Typeflo.io blog, you can also run live polls right in the comments. Turn on **Enable Polls** in your widget customization settings and a poll button appears in the comment toolbar. Polls take 2 to 10 options, results update live for everyone on the page, and you choose who can create polls and whether voting requires logging in. There is nothing else to install on Typeflo.io.

[Try the Live Polls demo](https://fastcomments.com/product/live-polls) or read the [Polls documentation](/guide-polls.html) to set them up.
