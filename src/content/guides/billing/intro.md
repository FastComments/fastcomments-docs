## Billing

This guide covers the parts of your FastComments bill that you manage yourself from the dashboard, starting with prepaid credit.

Your plan, payment method, and invoices live on the [Billing Info](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-info) page. Current usage against your plan's limits, and the cost breakdown for the next Flex invoice, is on the [Billing Analytics](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/analytics/billing) page, covered in the [Analytics guide](/guide-analytics.html#analytics-billing).
