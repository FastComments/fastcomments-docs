## Billing

This guide covers how FastComments bills you and the parts of your bill that you manage yourself from the dashboard: what the plans are, when your card is charged, how Flex usage is measured, invoices and tax details, what happens when a payment fails, prepaid credit, and who on your team can manage all of this.

Your plan, payment method, and invoices live on the [Billing Info](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-info) page. Current usage against your plan's limits, and the cost breakdown for the next Flex invoice, is on the [Billing Analytics](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/analytics/billing) page, covered in the [Analytics guide](/guide-analytics.html#analytics-billing).

Prices are not repeated here because they change. The [pricing page](https://fastcomments.com/traffic-pricing) always has the current rates.
