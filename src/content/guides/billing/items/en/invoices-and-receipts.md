Every charge, whether a plan renewal, a Flex month, or a plan change, produces an invoice. Invoices double as receipts once paid.

### Finding Invoices

Open [Billing History](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-history), or click `View Invoices` on the Billing Info page. The table lists each invoice with its date, billing period, invoice number, description, total, status (`Paid`, `Unpaid`, `Draft`, or `Void`), and due date, ten per page, newest first.

For each invoice:

- `View` opens the invoice on Stripe's hosted page, where you can also download it.
- `PDF` downloads the PDF directly.
- `Pay Now` appears instead of `View` on an unpaid invoice and takes you to Stripe's payment page for that invoice, where you can pay it directly.

Invoices are also emailed to your billing email when they are created. To have them go to a finance mailbox instead of the account owner, set the `Billing Email` in [Invoice Settings](#invoice-settings).

### What Is On The Invoice

The invoice shows the billing name and address and the VAT / Tax ID from [Invoice Settings](#invoice-settings), the line items, any prepaid [credit](#credits) that was applied, and the amount charged to your card. Flex invoices list one line per kind of usage, see [How Flex Usage Is Measured](#how-flex-usage-is-measured). For billing addresses in the European Union, Flex invoices are issued with 0% VAT and the footer `Reverse Charge Transaction: Customer to pay VAT`.

Changes to invoice settings apply to invoices created after you save them. An invoice that has already been issued cannot be edited, so set your company details and VAT / Tax ID before the next billing date.

### Accounts Billed Elsewhere

If your billing is handled externally, for example by invoice arranged with support, invoices are sent to you for payment rather than charged to a card and may not show in Billing History. Accounts installed through Shopify or BigCommerce are billed by that platform, and the Billing Info page links to the plan page there. Sub-accounts billed through a parent account have no invoices of their own; see [When You Are Charged](#when-you-are-charged).
