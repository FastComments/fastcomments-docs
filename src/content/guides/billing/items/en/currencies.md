Accounts are billed in US dollars by default. You can change the `Invoice Currency` in [Invoice Settings](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/invoice-settings) to any of the currencies in the list, which includes the euro, pound sterling, yen, Australian and Canadian dollars, Swiss francs, and a dozen more. How the choice is applied depends on your plan.

### Flex Invoices

Flex invoices can be issued in any currency on the list. Usage is priced in dollars and each line is converted at the exchange rate on the day the invoice is created, so the amount in your currency moves a little with the exchange rate from month to month. Changing the currency applies from the next invoice.

### Fixed Plan Subscriptions

`Creators` and `Pro` have their own prices in euros and pounds, so a new subscription started with `EUR` or `GBP` selected bills at the localized price in that currency. For any other currency, fixed plans bill in US dollars; the plan picker notes this.

A subscription's currency is fixed once it has started. If you change the invoice currency to `USD`, `EUR`, or `GBP` while on a fixed plan:

- Your current subscription keeps billing in its original currency until the end of the current period. The billing page shows `Your current subscription keeps billing in its original currency, even if your invoice currency setting is different.`
- At the end of the period the subscription is replaced by one in the new currency, at the price for that plan in that currency on that day, and renews from then on in the new currency. You do not need to re-enter your card.

Choosing any other currency leaves a fixed-plan subscription in US dollars.

Plan changes made while a subscription is active are priced in the subscription's currency, not the invoice setting.

### Credit

Prepaid credit is held in one currency, so the Credits page will not let you top up while your invoice currency differs from the currency of your existing balance. See [Accounts That Cannot Add Credit](#accounts-that-cannot-add-credit).

If you want an existing account switched to another currency and the options above do not fit, [contact support](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/help).
