Flex has no fixed caps, so a traffic spike or a runaway integration can produce a bigger invoice than you planned for. Spend limits let you cap the usage-based parts of the bill.

On the [Billing Info](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-info) page, the plan summary shows `Monthly spend limit`. Click `Edit` to open the dialog. You can either enter a `Target monthly max` and let FastComments set the individual limits to match, or set each limit yourself:

- `Page loads per month`
- `Comments stored`
- `SSO users`
- `API credits per month`
- `Small widget credits per month`

Leave a field empty to keep that kind of usage unlimited. The dialog shows the `Estimated monthly max` for the limits you entered, and the Flex minimum still applies.

### What A Limit Does

When a limit is reached, that kind of usage is blocked for the rest of the month. For page loads that means the widget stops loading comments and shows `This FastComments.com account has exceeded its limits.` until the month rolls over or you raise the limit. A raised limit takes effect within a few minutes.

The spend limit is not a hard cap on the whole invoice. It only covers the usage lines above. Seats and sites you add yourself, such as moderators or extra domains, are billed regardless, because you control those directly. Check the next invoice preview on [Billing Analytics](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/analytics/billing) at any time.

Only the `Account Owner` or a `Billing Admin` can change spend limits.
