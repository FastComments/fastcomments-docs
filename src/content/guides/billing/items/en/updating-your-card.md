There is no separate "edit card" screen. To replace the card on file, open [Billing Info](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-info), leave your plan as it is, enter the new card details under `Payment method`, and click `Save Info`.

The new card becomes the default for all future invoices. Nothing is charged when you save it, unless you have an unpaid invoice, in which case that invoice is collected with the new card: immediately on a fixed plan, and overnight on Flex. While your billing is in good standing the form says `You won't be charged now unless you change plans.` If your fixed-plan subscription has ended, saving a card starts a new one and the first period is charged, as on first sign-up.

If your bank requires you to confirm the card, a confirmation window opens after you click save. The card is only stored once the bank approves it.

### Who Can Do This

Only the `Account Owner` or a user with the `Billing Admin` permission can save a card or change the plan. Other users see `Only the account owner or a billing admin can change the plan or payment method.` If the `Settings` menu is greyed out or the billing page redirects you to the home page, you are logged in as a commenter or a user without billing access rather than the account owner. Log in with the account that created the site, or ask the owner to grant you the permission; see [Who Can Manage Billing](#who-can-manage-billing).

### Declined Or Blocked Cards

If the card is declined the page shows `Your card was declined. Check that the entered information is correct.` and nothing is saved. Check the number, expiry, CVC, and billing address, then try again. If the bank refused a confirmation, you see `Your bank did not approve the payment. Try again, or use a different card.`

Some banks block recurring online payments, or block a card after one declined attempt. In that case try another card, or pay an open invoice with `Pay Now` in [Billing History](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-history). If your bank will not allow automatic charges at all, [contact support](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/help) and we can send a payment link for the invoice instead.

Only cards are accepted. The `Country` list on the form contains the countries Stripe can bill; if yours is missing, contact support.
