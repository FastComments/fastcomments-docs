Credit is money held on your FastComments account, in your billing currency. It is applied automatically to your future invoices, including subscription renewals and Flex usage, before your card is charged.

Credit is prepayment, not a discount or a points system. Adding `$50` of credit means your next `$50` of invoices are paid from that credit instead of your card. The balance is held as a credit on your account in Stripe, our payment processor, and it stays on your account until an invoice uses it.

Some reasons to keep a credit balance:

- Prepay for a period in advance, for example when a finance department prefers one larger payment to a monthly card charge.
- Keep a buffer on a Flex plan so a busy month is covered before the invoice arrives.
- Pay from a card once, then let renewals draw from the balance.

### Where to Find It

Open [My Account -> Billing Info](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-info) and click `Manage Credits`, next to `View Invoices` and `Invoice Settings`. This opens the `Credits` page, which shows your current `Credit balance`, the `Add credit` form, and your `Past top-ups`.

Once you have a balance, the `Billing Info` page also shows a `Credit balance` line so you can see it without opening the Credits page.

### Who Can Add Credit

Only the `Account Owner` or a user with the `Billing Admin` permission can add credit, the same users who can change the plan or payment method. Other administrators do not see the `Manage Credits` button. See [Who Can Manage Billing](#who-can-manage-billing).
