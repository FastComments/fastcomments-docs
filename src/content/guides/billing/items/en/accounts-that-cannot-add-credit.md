Credit is for accounts that FastComments bills directly through Stripe. The `Manage Credits` button is not shown for:

- Accounts billed externally, for example by invoice outside the dashboard, or sub-accounts whose billing is handled by a parent account.
- Accounts installed through the Shopify App Store, which are billed through Shopify.
- Accounts installed through the BigCommerce App Marketplace, which are billed through BigCommerce.

For these accounts, payment is managed on the platform that bills you, and there is no balance on FastComments to top up.

### Currency Mismatch

Your credit balance is held in one currency, the one your account has already been invoiced in. If the invoice currency in `Invoice Settings` has been changed to a different currency, the Credits page explains the mismatch and does not let you add credit until the currencies agree. Set the invoice currency back in `Invoice Settings`, or [contact support](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/help) to switch currencies.
