You can cancel at any time from the [Cancel Subscription](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/cancel) page, also linked at the bottom of the [Billing Info](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-info) page as `Cancel Subscription`. Tick the confirmation box, optionally tell us what we could do better, and click `Yes, cancel my subscription`.

### What Cancelling Does

- Your Stripe subscription is cancelled immediately, not at the end of the period, so no further renewals are charged. On Flex, no further monthly invoices are created.
- Your account stays open and your data is kept, so you can come back later. Unless agreed otherwise we retain data for up to two years.
- Billing reminder emails stop.
- The widget stops showing comments on your site once the account is past its trial window. For an account older than sixty days that means right away, with the message `This FastComments account's billing information is more than sixty days out of date.`

Because cancelling is immediate, cancel when you are ready to stop using FastComments, not in advance. If you prepaid a year and want to keep the service until the paid period ends, [contact support](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/help) instead and we will set the account to stop at the end of the period.

An account that manages sub-accounts cannot be cancelled until the sub-accounts are deleted. The page tells you if this applies.

### Reactivating

Re-enter your billing info on the [Billing Info](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-info) page. On a fixed plan a new subscription starts and the first period is charged. On Flex nothing is charged until the end of the month. Any unpaid invoice from before is collected as well, overnight at the latest.

### Cancelling Is Not Deleting

Cancelling stops billing and hides the widget but keeps the account. Deleting removes the account and its data. If you want the account gone, cancel first and then delete it; see [Deleting Your Account](/guide-deleting-account.html).

### Still Being Billed After Cancelling?

Every FastComments account is billed separately, so a charge after you cancelled usually means there is a second account. Common causes are signing up twice with different emails or logins, creating one account on `fastcomments.com` and another on the EU deployment at `eu.fastcomments.com` (they are completely separate), or a plugin creating a new account during setup. Log in to the other account to cancel it, or [contact support](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/help) with the invoice number and we will find it.
