FastComments has two kinds of plans:

- **Fixed plans** (`FastComments for Creators` and `FastComments Pro`). A set monthly or yearly price with usage limits. Paying yearly is 15% cheaper than paying monthly.
- **Flex**. Usage-based. You pay for what you used during the month, with a small monthly minimum. Flex is always billed monthly, and includes debranding.

Current prices and limits are on the [pricing page](https://fastcomments.com/traffic-pricing), and the [pricing calculator](https://fastcomments.com/pricing-calculator) estimates a Flex bill from your expected traffic.

### Where Things Live

- [Billing Info](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-info): your plan, billing address, and card. Flex accounts also see a plan summary, the `Next Invoice Amount`, and the `Monthly spend limit` here.
- [Billing History](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-history) (the `View Invoices` button): every invoice, with `View`, `PDF`, and `Pay Now` for anything unpaid.
- [Invoice Settings](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/invoice-settings): billing email, company name, billing address, VAT / Tax ID, and invoice currency.
- [Billing Analytics](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/analytics/billing): usage against your limits this month, and on Flex the line-by-line cost of the next invoice. See the [Analytics guide](/guide-analytics.html#analytics-billing).
- `Manage Credits`: prepaid credit, covered in the [Credits](#credits) section below.

All of these are reached from `Settings` in the dashboard, or from the avatar menu in the top right.

Payments are processed by Stripe. FastComments never stores your card details.
