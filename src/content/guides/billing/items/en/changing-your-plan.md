You change plans from the [Billing Info](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-info) page. You never need to create a new account to change plans, and your comments, users, and configuration stay exactly as they are.

### Between Fixed Plans, Or Monthly To Yearly

On a fixed plan the Billing Info page shows the plan picker. Choose `Pay Monthly` or `Pay Annually`, select the plan you want, re-enter your card details, and submit.

The change is prorated by Stripe and applied immediately: you are credited for the unused part of your current period, charged for the rest of the period on the new plan, and the resulting invoice is charged to your card right away. If your bank asks you to confirm the payment, your plan only changes once the confirmation completes. A failed payment leaves your plan as it was.

A plan is shown as `Unavailable` when your usage this month already exceeds one of its limits. Click the question mark next to it to see which limit.

### Switching To Flex

Select `Flex` in the plan picker and save your billing info. Nothing is charged at that point. Your fixed subscription ends immediately and usage is billed from then on, at the end of the month, as described in [When You Are Charged](#when-you-are-charged).

Any time left on a fixed plan you already paid for is not refunded automatically. If you prepaid a year and want to move to Flex, [contact support](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/help) first so the remainder can be sorted out.

### Switching From Flex To A Fixed Plan

Flex accounts do not see the plan picker. Compare the [fixed-price plans](https://fastcomments.com/traffic-pricing?show-all=true), then [contact support](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/help) and we will switch you.

### Fixed Plan Limits

Fixed plans include a monthly allowance of page loads, comments, API credits and so on, plus caps on sites, team members, and moderators. Monthly allowances reset at the start of each calendar month (UTC). Comments count when they are submitted, so a comment you later delete still counts for that month.

When an allowance is used up the widget shows `This FastComments.com account has exceeded its limits.` until the month rolls over. To keep going, move to a bigger plan or to Flex, which has no fixed caps. The [Billing Analytics](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/analytics/billing) page shows how much of each allowance you have used.

### Custom Pricing

Larger communities, agencies, and accounts with unusual usage patterns (for example many SSO users who rarely comment) can ask for a custom package or a fixed-cost agreement. [Contact support](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/help) with your expected usage.
