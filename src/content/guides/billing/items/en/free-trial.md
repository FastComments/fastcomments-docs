Every new account starts with a 30 day free trial. No card is needed to start, and nothing is charged until you save a payment method.

### Trial Limits

While your account has no valid billing info, trial limits apply regardless of which plan you have picked. They are lower than any paid plan, for example a few hundred comments and a handful of team members, moderators, and sites. You can see the exact numbers, and how close you are to them, on the [Billing Analytics](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/analytics/billing) page. If you hit a limit the widget shows `This FastComments.com account has exceeded its limits.`

Saving a payment method on the [Billing Info](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-info) page removes the trial limits right away and applies the limits of the plan you chose. If you need the trial limits lifted before you decide, [contact support](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/help).

### Domains Added Automatically

During the trial, sites are added to your account automatically the first time the widget loads from a new domain, so you do not have to configure anything to get started. You get an email each time this happens, with a link to turn the behaviour off. On Flex this stops as soon as billing info is saved, since additional sites are billed. See [Multiple Sites](/guide-multiple-sites.html#domain-limits).

### Trial Reminders

About halfway through the trial, users with the `Billing Admin` permission get a reminder email. Once the trial has ended without billing info, reminder emails go out every three days for up to sixty days. Every reminder has an unsubscribe link, and they stop as soon as you add billing info or cancel.

### When The Trial Ends

Nothing is charged automatically at the end of the trial, because there is no card on file. Instead:

- For the first thirty days after the trial, the widget keeps working. Site administrators see a notice in the widget that billing info is out of date and comments will be hidden after sixty days. Visitors do not see it.
- After that, comments are hidden and visitors see `This FastComments account's billing information is more than sixty days out of date.`

Adding a payment method on the [Billing Info](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-info) page fixes both immediately. Your data is kept, so nothing is lost by waiting.

If you picked Flex, you are not charged when you save your card. Flex invoices are issued on the last day of each month, and usage in the calendar month you signed up in is never billed, so at the earliest your first invoice arrives at the end of the month after you signed up. See [When You Are Charged](#when-you-are-charged).
