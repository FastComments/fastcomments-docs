You do not have to do anything to use your credit. When an invoice is created for your account, the available credit is applied to it first, and only the remainder, if any, is charged to your card.

This covers every invoice FastComments issues for your account:

- Monthly or yearly subscription renewals.
- Flex usage charges, billed at the end of each period.
- Plan changes that produce an invoice.

If your balance is larger than the invoice, the whole invoice is paid from credit and the rest stays on your account for the next one. If the balance is smaller, the credit is used up and your card is charged the difference.

Credit is applied to invoices only. It does not change your plan, your limits, or the prices you pay, and it is not a spend limit. Flex spend limits are configured separately, see [Flex Spend Limits](#flex-spend-limits).

Each invoice shows the credit that was applied to it. You can review invoices from `View Invoices` on the Billing Info page.

### Checking Your Balance

The `Credits` page always shows your current `Credit balance`. When it is above zero, the [Billing Info](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/billing-info) page shows a `Credit balance` line as well.

### Refunds

Credit is prepayment for your FastComments account and is used by your future invoices. If you need help with a balance you no longer need, [contact support](https://fastcomments.com/auth/my-account/help).
